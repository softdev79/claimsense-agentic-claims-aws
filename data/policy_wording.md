# NorthStar General Insurance (fictional) — Private Car Package Policy Wording

> Teaching sample written by Shivank Agrawal. Simplified from the structure of a standard
> Indian private car package policy and the India Motor Tariff. Not a real insurance contract.
> Every clause starts with "## [CLAUSE-ID] Title" so the agent can retrieve and cite it.

## [OD-1] Section I — Own damage cover
The Company will indemnify the insured against loss of or damage to the insured vehicle and its
accessories caused by: fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning; burglary, housebreaking or theft;
riot and strike; earthquake; flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, cyclone, inundation or landslide;
accidental external means (for example a collision with another vehicle, a wall, a tree or an animal);
malicious act; and while in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator or air.
Section I applies only to policies issued as a Package (Comprehensive) policy.

## [OD-2] Basis of claim settlement
In case of partial loss the Company will pay the reasonable cost of repair of the damaged parts as
assessed by the Company's surveyor, after deducting depreciation on parts as per clause DEP-1 and the
compulsory deductible as per clause DED-1. The liability of the Company shall not exceed the Insured
Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle. Labour charges are paid in full without depreciation.

## [OD-3] Total loss and constructive total loss
Where the aggregate cost of retrieval and repair of the vehicle, as assessed by the surveyor, exceeds
75% of the Insured Declared Value, the vehicle shall be treated as a Constructive Total Loss (CTL).
Total loss and CTL claims are settled on the basis of IDV and require approval by a claims officer.

## [DEP-1] Depreciation schedule for parts
Depreciation is deducted from the cost of replaced parts as follows:
- Rubber, nylon and plastic parts, tyres, tubes and batteries: 50%
- Fibre glass components: 30%
- Glass parts: Nil
- Metal parts, based on the age of the vehicle from the date of first registration:
  - not exceeding 6 months: Nil
  - exceeding 6 months but not exceeding 1 year: 5%
  - exceeding 1 year but not exceeding 2 years: 10%
  - exceeding 2 years but not exceeding 3 years: 15%
  - exceeding 3 years but not exceeding 4 years: 25%
  - exceeding 4 years but not exceeding 5 years: 35%
  - exceeding 5 years but not exceeding 10 years: 40%
  - exceeding 10 years: 50%

## [DED-1] Compulsory deductible
Every own damage claim is subject to a compulsory deductible (the amount the insured bears):
Rs. 1,000 for private cars with engine capacity up to 1500 cc, and Rs. 2,000 for private cars with
engine capacity above 1500 cc.

## [ADD-ZD] Add-on — Zero Depreciation cover
Where the Zero Depreciation add-on is shown in the policy schedule, no depreciation under clause DEP-1
will be deducted on parts replaced. The compulsory deductible under DED-1 still applies.

## [LIM-1] Limitations as to use
The policy covers use of the vehicle only for social, domestic and pleasure purposes and for the
insured's own business. The policy does NOT cover use for hire or reward, carriage of passengers for
payment (including rides booked through ride-hailing or app-based taxi platforms), carriage of goods
(other than samples or personal luggage), organised racing, pace making, reliability trials, speed
testing, or use for driving tuition.

## [TP-1] Section II — Liability to third parties
The Company will indemnify the insured against legal liability for death or bodily injury to any
person and for damage to third-party property caused by the use of the insured vehicle. A policy issued
as "Liability Only" (Third Party) covers Section II only and does not cover damage to the insured's
own vehicle.

## [EXC-1] General exception — geographical area
The Company shall not be liable for any accident, loss or damage occurring outside India.

## [EXC-2] Exception — wear and tear and breakdowns
The Company shall not be liable for normal wear and tear, depreciation, or mechanical or electrical
breakdown, failures or breakages of the vehicle, including engine seizure caused by lack of
lubrication or oil starvation, unless such damage is the direct result of an accident covered under
OD-1.

## [EXC-3] Exception — consequential loss
The Company shall not be liable for any consequential loss, such as loss of use of the vehicle, taxi
fare, or loss of income while the vehicle is under repair.

## [EXC-4] Exception — driving under the influence
The Company shall not be liable for any accidental loss or damage suffered whilst the person driving
the vehicle is under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs. A breath analyser reading or blood
test above the legal limit of 30 mg of alcohol per 100 ml of blood, as recorded by the police, is
treated as driving under the influence.

## [EXC-5] Exception — driver without a valid licence
The Company shall not be liable for any accidental loss or damage suffered whilst the vehicle is driven
by any person who does not hold an effective (valid, unexpired) driving licence for that class of
vehicle at the time of the accident.

## [EXC-6] Exception — use outside limitations
The Company shall not be liable for any accidental loss or damage suffered whilst the vehicle is being
used otherwise than in accordance with the Limitations as to Use in clause LIM-1.

## [EXC-7] Exception — war and nuclear perils
The Company shall not be liable for any loss or damage directly or indirectly caused by war, invasion,
act of foreign enemy, hostilities, civil war, rebellion, nuclear weapons material or ionising radiation.

## [COND-1] Condition — notice of loss
Notice shall be given to the Company immediately, and in any case within 7 days, of any accident, loss
or damage. Delayed intimation may be condoned by the Company if the insured gives a valid reason.
Late intimation alone is not a ground to reject a claim; it is reviewed by a claims officer.

## [COND-2] Condition — safeguarding the vehicle
The insured shall take all reasonable steps to safeguard the vehicle from loss or damage. If the vehicle
is left unattended after an accident, any further damage resulting from it is not payable.

## [COND-3] Condition — fraud
If any claim is in any respect fraudulent, or if any false declaration is made or used in support of
it, or if any fraudulent means are used by the insured or anyone acting on the insured's behalf to
obtain any benefit under this policy, all benefits under the policy shall be forfeited.

## [COND-4] Condition — police report
In case of theft, malicious damage, or an accident involving a third party injury or death, the insured
shall lodge a First Information Report (FIR) with the police and furnish a copy to the Company.
